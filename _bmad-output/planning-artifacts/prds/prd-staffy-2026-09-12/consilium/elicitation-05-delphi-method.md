# Advanced Elicitation 05 — Delphi Method

**Target:** accepted Montagio consilium synthesis after four elicitation passes  
**Status:** proposal only; not yet applied to the synthesis or PRD  
**Method:** independent estimates → anonymized reveal → revise → converge  
**Panel lenses:** field-service domain/safety, two-sided marketplace operations, conversational/omnichannel UX

## Process and evidence boundary

Three experts independently rated ten propositions without reading each other's Delphi reports. They then received the same anonymized distribution and five proposed convergence statements and independently revised their positions.

This is structured expert judgment based on the current artifacts. It strengthens internal decision quality but does not replace customer interviews, legal/accounting approval, current market-price evidence, live supply validation or paid-order cohorts.

## Round 2 convergence

All three experts accepted C1–C5. Four statements received confidence 5 from every or nearly every lens; C5 received 5, 5 and 4 because high-level grouping could hide acceptance criteria.

### C1 — policy outcomes, not seven workflow systems

P1–P6 remain customer-facing policy outcomes that may occur in sequence. P7 becomes a priority safety interrupt available from any state: it stops sales, gives only approved safe guidance, creates/updates the human handoff and may later lead to P5, P6 or another explicitly re-authorised route. Architecture is not required to implement seven separate workflow engines.

### C2 — two commitments, one delta-focused final confirmation

The two distinct commitments remain mandatory:

1. the customer confirms the understood task and preliminary route;
2. the customer confirms the named executor and final execution terms.

The final view highlights material changes rather than repeating the intake. It always shows the named executor, final price/range, payee and work/guarantee role, with full scope available in one action. Material changes requiring explicit reconfirmation include scope/exclusions, price/range, executor, window, material route/cost, payee, warranty role and cancellation terms.

### C3 — both business proofs before Montagio scale

UJ-M1 and UJ-M2 prove different things and both must pass before Montagio increases autonomous commercial exposure. M1-first remains the recommended path because it isolates the Staffy customer journey; the order may change if the partner store and supply are demonstrably ready earlier.

For this purpose, a scale event includes raising autonomy, increasing paid/campaign traffic, exposing additional services or zones, enabling an unattended partner channel, or promising B2B response commitments. A single successful order validates a path, not repeatable business performance.

The approved Product Brief already requires 20 controlled end-to-end dialogues and 100 target live contacts across the Staffy pilot evidence plan, with zero critical unconfirmed actions, duplicate orders, dangerous advice or lost handoff context. The PRD must allocate a meaningful Montagio M1/M2 sample before the test starts rather than inventing a successful split after seeing results.

### C4 — premium strategy is fixed; the exact percentile is tested

Montagio's no-dumping strategy and evidence-linked consultative selling are fixed product principles. The 60–75th-percentile target is a versioned `service × market` commercial hypothesis with source, comparison scope, observation date and applicability boundary. Staffy uses the approved price returned by policy and cannot improvise a markup.

If the service does not produce positive contribution margin in the chosen upper-market band, the response is to improve the value/supply model, change the offer or withhold that service—not automatically discount it merely to lift conversion.

### C5 — five pilot outcomes, not five new subsystems

Must-now requirements are organised and accepted through five outcomes:

| Outcome | Minimum meaning |
|---|---|
| Safe intake | AI/source disclosure, consent, mode/safety interrupt, no-repeat context, deduplication and one canonical case/order |
| Safe promise | Valid route and price policy, current supply readiness, known unknowns and two distinct customer commitments |
| Safe fulfilment | One eligible executor, decision-ready offer, atomic assignment, versioned scope/evidence and stop-work for changes |
| Safe close/recovery | Separate operational/payment/fee/claim states, acknowledged owner, honest updates, bounded AI actions and contest path |
| Business proof | Both M1 and M2 live evidence, including conversion, real cash/commission, contribution margin, manual minutes, no-supply, re-quote, complaints and recovery |

These are delivery and acceptance groupings. They are not names for new services, workflow engines or platforms. The pilot uses the existing canonical order plus the minimum number of statuses, manual flags, exception records and audit events.

## Residual uncertainties to carry into PRD

The panel found no remaining material disagreement about product direction. It did not resolve these evidence-dependent details:

1. the precise Montagio allocation within the 20-controlled/100-live Staffy evidence plan;
2. numerical thresholds for conversion, no-supply, re-quote, manual minutes, complaints, cash closure and trust;
3. the current comparable-market source and freshness period for each service's percentile;
4. who owns the manual fallback and acknowledgement target for each safety/recovery class;
5. the smallest useful set and granularity of `accept`, `intake_only/no_promises`, `human_only` and `paused` controls.

These are PRD open items or pre-pilot operating decisions. They are not permission to omit measurement, ownership or safe fallback.

## Proposed changes if accepted

1. Rename the seven “final routes” as policy outcomes; model P7 explicitly as a priority safety interrupt.
2. Define the mandatory final-confirmation fields and material delta list above.
3. Preserve M1-first as a recommendation but require both M1 and M2 before a defined Montagio scale event.
4. Make the exact premium percentile a versioned testable service×market hypothesis without weakening the no-dumping strategy.
5. Reorganise Must-now into the five outcome groups while preserving every underlying control and traceable acceptance criterion.
6. Limit pilot mechanisms to simple flags, linked exception records, owner/acknowledgement states and audit unless observed load or failure justifies more.
7. Carry the five residual details as explicit PRD open items with an owner/revisit condition; do not fabricate numbers during synthesis.

## Explicit non-changes

- Do not remove any accepted safety, consent, payment, recovery or premium-selling requirement.
- Do not make UJ-M2 optional merely because UJ-M1 succeeds.
- Do not turn the price percentile into a discount target or give the model authority to alter price.
- Do not present Delphi agreement as proof of demand, usability, profitability or legal validity.
- Do not build a platform subsystem solely because an outcome group or P-code has a name.
