- (event) Create intent confirmed. Primary inputs: completed Staffy Forge, 94-source technical research, original AI-search chat, and current user decisions. Stakes: product foundation for first paid pilots and downstream PRD; exact external business traction remains unverified.
- (decision) User selected Coaching path and requested Party Mode where it materially improves decisions; use roundtables for ICP/buyer, product promise, v1 scope, and success criteria, not for routine clarification.
- (decision by user) Две отрасли запускаются с самого начала, а не последовательно. Автозапчасти/ABCP — внешний дизайн-партнёр и проверка готовности независимого клиента платить. Montagio — собственный действующий reference customer и проверка второй отрасли: Staffy должен работать там экспертным сотрудником по телефону и в текстовых чатах. Montagio проверяет полезность продукта, переносимость ядра и эксплуатацию в реальном бизнесе, но сам по себе не доказывает внешний платёжный спрос.
- (decision by user) Для Montagio принят outcome-based boundary: Staffy самостоятельно ведёт обращение до наиболее далёкого безопасного обязательства клиента. Для стандартной услуги это подтверждённый заказ/визит; для сложной — согласованный замер или диагностика; для опасного, нестандартного или недостаточно определённого случая — бесшовная передача человеку с полным контекстом. Назначение конкретного мастера не является обязательным универсальным условием завершения и зависит от правил услуги и доступности.
- (decision by user) Primary launch economic buyer: owner, founder, or general manager of a small/medium business whose inbound phone and chat conversations directly affect revenue. Heads of sales/operations are internal champions; managers supervise exceptions and handoffs; IT staff and integrators influence feasibility but are not the primary buyer or positioning target. Buying triggers include missed contacts, slow response, difficult/expensive hiring, inconsistent manager quality, and need for 24/7 handling.
- (decision by user) Core product promise accepted: Staffy gives a business a full-fledged AI employee for phone and text chats that understands its role, conducts complex conversations, and performs permitted actions in the business's existing systems. Launch means configuring an employee and attaching reusable integrations, not developing a separate product/core for each customer. If a needed connector does not exist, the Staffy team may build it once as a reusable platform asset; therefore 'easy/no-code setup' applies to supported capabilities and must not be presented as zero engineering for every previously unseen system.
- (decision by user) First proof scope accepted: two real AI employees are required in v1—an autoparts sales employee for Oleg and an expert service employee for Montagio. Each must operate through one phone line and at least one real text channel, share the same Staffy core without vertical forks, and execute real business-system actions rather than only consult. Telegram, MAX, Jivo, website chat, and mobile-app channels remain part of the target platform, but simultaneous availability of every channel is not a blocker for the first proof.
- (decision by user) First external-pilot text channel for Oleg's autoparts store: Jivo, because it currently brings the largest volume of target customer conversations. This tests bring-your-own-inbox on real traffic. Working first text channel for Montagio remains its owned website chat; this choice still needs explicit confirmation or can be treated as the default absent an objection.
- (decision by user) Pilot proof system accepted. Stage 1: 20 controlled end-to-end dialogues per vertical across voice/text and exception paths. Stage 2: 100 eligible live customer contacts per vertical under supervision. Universal safety gate: zero critical unauthorized or unconfirmed actions, duplicate orders, unsafe recommendations, or context loss on handoff. Autoparts economic outcome: contribution margin per 100 eligible contacts no worse than the supervising manager/comparison cohort. Montagio outcome: correct safe next customer commitment plus positive contribution margin on completed orders, with an initial parallel manual cohort because no historical live-order baseline is yet verified. These are pilot acceptance criteria, not claims of achieved performance.
- (decision by user) Commercial packaging accepted without fixed prices: one-time launch/process configuration/integration fee; recurring subscription per AI employee (one role, permissions, knowledge, and cross-channel identity); variable charge for actual telephony and AI consumption. Adding a channel to the same identity does not automatically create another employee. A missing reusable connector may be commissioned separately, but it becomes a shared Staffy platform asset rather than a customer-specific core fork.
- (decision by user) 2–3 year setup vision accepted: a business using a supported industry pack and connectors can launch an AI employee independently or with a consultant within one working day, configuring role, permissions, processes, and knowledge rather than programming. A previously unsupported system or process is handled by Staffy or a certified partner; its connector/capability becomes a reusable platform asset. Product complexity stays inside Staffy rather than being exposed to the client.
- (decision by user) First-release non-goals accepted: no promise to support every industry/system without an existing connector; no requirement to launch every channel simultaneously; no replacement of client CRM/ERP/ABCP/Montagio/Jivo/telephony; no autonomous action outside configured permissions/confirmation policy; no per-client core fork; no expectation of resolving 100% of contacts without human handoff; no open connector/skills marketplace in v1. The release must still conduct complex dialogue and perform authorized business actions in both initial industries over voice and selected text channels.
