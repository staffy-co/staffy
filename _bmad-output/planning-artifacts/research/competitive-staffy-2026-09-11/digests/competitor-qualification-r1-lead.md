# Competitor qualification — round 1 (lead)

Accessed: 2026-09-11

## Findings

1. **Claim:** Voxagent is a qualified direct Russian competitor rather than an anonymous landing page: a current public offer identifies ООО «НОУКОД» (ОГРН 1237700304742, ИНН 9714009485) and contracts for design, setup, integrations, testing, operation and support of voice AI agents that update CRM, create requests and transfer calls.
   - Source: https://www.voxagent.ru/docs/public-offer
   - Publisher: Voxagent / ООО «НОУКОД»
   - Pub date: 2026-06-16
   - Confidence: high
   - Class: competitor_qualification

2. **Claim:** Voxagent's offer makes managed implementation and ongoing service part of the product. It also places responsibility for lawful personal-data grounds and call-recording consent on the customer, disclaims accuracy/completeness guarantees, limits damages to 20% of the relevant billing period and allows pricing changes. These terms expose trust, evaluation and risk-allocation as competitive product dimensions.
   - Source: https://www.voxagent.ru/docs/public-offer
   - Publisher: Voxagent / ООО «НОУКОД»
   - Pub date: 2026-06-16
   - Confidence: high
   - Class: packaging

3. **Claim:** Replica's own company card identifies the contracting entity as ИП Шандульский Илья Олегович (ОГРНИП 324665800003784, software publishing activity). This qualifies the product as a Russian commercial entrant, while not validating its performance claims.
   - Source: https://storage.yandexcloud.net/replica-production/documents/company_card.pdf
   - Publisher: Replica
   - Pub date: current document, accessed 2026-09-11
   - Confidence: high
   - Class: competitor_qualification

4. **Claim:** Stexa's current “About” page is internally inconsistent: it says the company is still being formally registered, while also claiming millions of monthly minutes, Russian software-registry inclusion, FSTEC certification, government suitability, 99.9% enterprise SLA, and precise accuracy/latency figures. No official registry/FSTEC source was found in the scoped search. Stexa should be tracked as an unqualified emerging offer, not treated as evidence of established traction or certified compliance.
   - Source: https://stexa.ru/about/
   - Publisher: Stexa
   - Pub date: current page, accessed 2026-09-11
   - Confidence: high for the internal contradiction; low for every uncorroborated performance/compliance claim
   - Class: source_quality

5. **Claim:** Myrolit's footer identifies a Ukrainian legal entity (ТОВ «МИРОЛІТ»), so its Russian-language vertical pages demonstrate accessible product competition but not a Russia-local compliant supplier without further evidence.
   - Source: https://myrolit.com/ru/resheniya/stroitelstvo/
   - Publisher: Myrolit
   - Pub date: current page, accessed 2026-09-11
   - Confidence: medium
   - Class: competitor_qualification

## Leads

- Promote Voxagent and Replica into the core comparison set.
- Downgrade Stexa claims unless official registry/certificate/customer evidence is produced.
- Keep Myrolit in an adjacent/international column rather than the Russia-local core set.
- Compare contracts and responsibility allocation, not only demos and feature pages.

## Contradictions and negative findings

- “Russian-hosted” and “152-FZ compliant” are common vendor labels, but the contractual allocation of controller/processor duties and liability differs materially.
- No official evidence was located in this pass for Stexa's software-registry or FSTEC certification claim.
