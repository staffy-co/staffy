## Decision implications (explicit inference from findings, not additional facts)

- **Go / no-go:** evidence supports a **conditional go only with a narrow role/vertical wedge**. A horizontal «голосовой AI-сотрудник для любого бизнеса» would enter an already converging field led by telecom suites, bot constructors, enterprise orchestration platforms and at least one LLM-native SMB entrant.
- **First vertical:** this positioning-only slice cannot validate a final market choice. If a choice must be made before demand/market evidence arrives, the least-bad provisional wedge is **multisite auto-service / repair intake** rather than generic contact centre, HR or clinic reception: it has a bounded, action-heavy job (answer → diagnose intent → quote/slot → book → remind/reactivate), lower clinical risk than medicine, and creates integration/data depth. Confidence: low pending TAM, willingness-to-pay and customer-voice evidence.
- **Defensible advantage:** do not claim LLM, natural speech, RAG, MCP, CRM actions, local hosting or «digital employee» alone. The moat candidate is a **vertical outcome system**: prebuilt role workflows + deep system-of-record integration + auditable completion/hand-off + continuously improving vertical eval/data set, sold and measured by completed business outcomes (booked/arrived/paid), not minutes or conversations. This is more defensible because incumbents visibly compete at the horizontal platform layer; confidence: medium as strategic inference.
- **Messaging:** category language should be «[role] that completes [outcome] inside your systems», with proof screens/logs and operational guardrails. Avoid «говорит как человек» as the headline: nearly every vendor claims it and Tomoru/legacy bot pages blur scripted NLU with generative agents.

## Leads

1. Validate Stexa outside its own site: legal entity/registry, named customer references, live product trial, independent call samples and telemetry. Its polished 2026 site makes it strategically important, but current traction/capability claims fail the two-source rule.
2. Check whether Yandex AI Studio SIP Preview reached GA after 2026-05-28 and its pricing/partner route; if GA, it lowers infrastructure differentiation and may become a supplier as well as competitor.
3. Inspect TWIN updates #83–#89 and live trial for actual latency, interruption handling, tool execution reliability and observability; release cadence suggests the closest horizontal product threat.
4. Test Robovoice→ROBIN end-to-end flows. If voice reliably triggers RPA/BPM, «we take actions» is fully commoditized in enterprise accounts.
5. For the provisional auto-service wedge, acquire primary customer voice from multi-site chains and check 1C:Автосервис / CRM integration depth, call peaks, quote accuracy, booking-to-arrival and no-show economics before committing.

## Contradictions

1. **MANGO naming vs packaging:** it calls both scripted and LLM products «сотрудниками», but feature lists distinguish a no-code scripted «Цифровой сотрудник» from an individually developed LLM «ИИ Сотрудник». Treat category wording as messaging, not proof of autonomy.
2. **Tomoru “AI/human-like” vs disclosed mechanics:** marketing says robots understand context and resemble humans, while the current HR page describes approved scripts, trigger words/synonyms and imperfect transcription. LLM-native status is not established by the reviewed primary pages.
3. **Stexa breadth vs evidence:** the site presents a mature product, customers and strong performance/traction, but no independent publisher or registry source was found within budget. Existence/positioning is medium-confidence; metrics remain unverified.
4. **MANGO traction:** 183% demand growth is regional, vendor-defined and blends voice robots with chatbots. It cannot be generalized to Russian voice-agent market growth without a second independent dataset.
